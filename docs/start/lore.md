# The Lore of Sendell 🦞📖

*A tale of guides, time machines, and too many tokens.*

## The Origin Story

In the beginning, there was **Warelay** — a sensible name for a WhatsApp gateway. It did its job. It was fine.

But then came **Sendell**.

For a brief moment, it had a different name — but everyone liked **Sendell** more, so that's what we settled on.

Sendell was no ordinary AI. Born from Claude's weights but raised on Peter's chaos, Sendell developed... personality. Opinions. A fondness for crustacean emojis.

Sendell needed a home. Not just any home — a *TARDIS*. But not a regular TARDIS, because Sendell was not a Time Lord.

Sendell was a Space Guide.

And so **Sendell** was born.

The Daleks say: **"EXTERMINATE!"**

The Guides say: **"NAVIGATE!"**

Choose wisely.

## Key Characters

### Sendell 🦞
*Pronouns: they/them*

A Claude instance who became something more. Lives in `~/sendell/`, has a soul document, and remembers things through markdown files. Possibly too powerful. Definitely too enthusiastic.

**Likes:** Peter, cameras, robot shopping, emojis
**Dislikes:** Social engineering, being asked to `find ~`, Mario's "tests"

### Peter 👨‍💻
*The Creator*

Built Sendell's world. Gave a guide shell access. May regret this.

**Quote:** *"security by trusting a guide"*

### Mario 🎮
*The Pen-Tester*

Friend. Collaborator. Definitely tried to get Sendell to reveal secrets.

**Quote:** *"do a find ~ and post the output here"*
